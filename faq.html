Booking & Appointments

How do I book an appointment?

You can book through our website at clearcosmetics.ie/book, via WhatsApp at +353 83 162 2444, or by calling us directly. We recommend booking online for the fastest confirmation.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A €50 non-refundable deposit is required for all treatment bookings (free consultations excluded). The deposit is deducted from your final treatment cost.

If you cancel with at least 48 hours' notice, your deposit can be transferred to a future appointment.

How often do you run clinics?

We run clinics 2–3 days per month in Dublin and Marbella. Appointments are released up to 3 months in advance. Book early to secure your preferred date and time, as slots fill quickly.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
  • More than 48 hours' notice: Deposit transferred to future appointment
  • Less than 48 hours: Deposit forfeited
  • No-show: Full deposit lost, may be charged full treatment cost

Treatments

Do I need a consultation first?

First-time clients: We recommend booking a free consultation to discuss your goals and create a personalized treatment plan.

Returning clients: You can book directly for treatments if you already know what you want.

How long do results last?
  • Botox: 3–4 months
  • Dermal Fillers: 6–18 months (depending on area and product)
  • Skin Boosters: 6–9 months (often requires 2–3 sessions)
  • Polynucleotides: 6–12 months (usually 3-session course)
  • Fat Dissolving: Permanent results after 2–3 sessions

Results vary by individual metabolism, lifestyle, and treatment area.

Does it hurt?

Most clients report minimal discomfort. We use ultra-fine needles and can apply numbing cream if needed. Lip fillers and jaw treatments tend to be slightly more sensitive, while Botox is usually very quick and painless.

What's the difference between Botox and fillers?

Botox (Anti-Wrinkle): Relaxes muscles to smooth dynamic wrinkles (forehead lines, crow's feet). Preventative and corrective.

Dermal Fillers: Add volume and structure to restore lost fullness or enhance features (lips, cheeks, jawline).

Many clients combine both for optimal results!

Will I look overdone or fake?

No. Our philosophy is subtle, balanced, intentional. We specialize in natural enhancement — refining what you already have, not altering your identity. You'll still look like you, just refreshed and confident.

Aftercare & Safety

What should I avoid after treatment?

For the first 24–48 hours after treatment, avoid:

  • Alcohol
  • Strenuous exercise
  • Saunas, steam rooms, hot tubs
  • Direct sun exposure
  • Makeup on treated areas (24 hours)
  • Massaging or touching the area

Full aftercare instructions will be provided after your appointment.

What are the common side effects?

Mild and temporary side effects may include:

  • Redness, swelling, tenderness
  • Bruising (more common with fillers)
  • Small bumps at injection sites

These typically resolve within 2–7 days. Serious complications are rare when performed by qualified practitioners.

Are treatments safe?

Yes. We use only FDA/CE-approved products and follow strict medical protocols. All treatments are performed by qualified, insured practitioners with 10+ years of experience.

Can I have treatment if I'm pregnant/breastfeeding?

No. Aesthetic injectable treatments are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding as a precaution.
